LINKA COMMUNITY GUIDELINES & ToS
                                Effective Date: 10/13/2026

Ensuring a safe, respectful, and functional global feed for everyone. By using Linka, 
you agree to strictly follow these rules.

SECTION 1 — COMMUNITY SAFETY & CONDUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Hate Speech & Discrimination
    Promoting, encouraging, or displaying hate speech, racial slurs, homophobia, Nazism, 
    extremism, or dehumanizing language is STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
    Penalty: Permanent IP & Account Ban.

1.2 Harassment, Doxxing & Threats
    Do not harass, stalk, threaten physical harm, or share non-consensual personally 
    identifiable information (PII/Doxxing) of any individual.
    Penalty: Permanent IP & Account Ban.

1.3 Explicit Content (NSFW, Gore & Child Safety)
    Posting pornographic material, sexually explicit media, animal cruelty, or shocking/gore 
    content is strictly forbidden. Content depicting or promoting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) 
    will be reported directly to law enforcement authorities.
    Penalty: Permanent IP & Account Ban + Legal Escalation.

1.4 Self-Harm & Violence
    Glorifying, encouraging, or displaying self-harm, suicide, or violent extremism is prohibited.
    Penalty: Account Ban / Content Removal.


SECTION 2 — PLATFORM INTEGRITY & BOT SDK RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Spam, Phishing & Malicious Activity
    Automated mass-posting, sending malicious links, phishing scams, or flooding the feed 
    with unauthenticated traffic is forbidden.
    Penalty: Permanent Account Ban + API Token Revocation.

2.2 Ban Evasion
    Creating new accounts or proxy routes to bypass an active temporary or permanent ban 
    is strictly prohibited.
    Penalty: Immediate Subnet / Range IP Ban.

2.3 Bot Ecosystem & API Usage
    All automated bots interacting with the Linka API must use official tokens issued via 
    the `linkaBotsSdk`. Unofficial scraping, user-account automation (self-bots), or API 
    abuse meant to bypass rate limits will result in blacklisting.
    Penalty: Developer Key Revocation + Account Termination.

2.4 Account Fraud & Impersonation
    Impersonating other users, staff members, or entities, as well as buying or selling 
    Linka accounts or usernames, is prohibited.
    Penalty: Account Termination.


SECTION 3 — LEGAL & COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Copyright & Intellectual Property
    Sharing pirated material, game hacks/cheats, or content that infringes upon third-party 
    intellectual property is not allowed.
    Penalty: Content Removal / Repeated Offender Ban.

3.2 Illegal Goods & Services
    Using Linka to facilitate, organize, or conduct illegal activities, including the sale 
    of regulated goods, weapons, or illegal substances, is strictly forbidden.
    Penalty: Permanent IP Ban + Legal Escalation.
