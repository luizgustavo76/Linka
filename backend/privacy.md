# Linka Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

We, the Linka development team, wish to clarify that user privacy is a fundamental pillar of security.

**External Networks & Tokens:** Regarding measures applied to external networks (such as Threads, Mastodon, Bluesky, Reddit, and X), there is a strict requirement that no authentication tokens of any kind are stored on our servers. All external bridge requests are processed statelessly or using local client-side parameters.

**Data Deletion Instructions:** Because Linka does not store external user tokens or personal data from third-party networks on its servers, revoking access directly from your Threads/Meta account settings immediately terminates any bridge interaction. For native Linka accounts, users can request full account and post deletion at any time via the app settings or by contacting `linka.plataform@gmail.com`.
